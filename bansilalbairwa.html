About Me

I am an academic professional with a career in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Currently working as Assistant Professor at REVA University, I have over four years of experience in teaching. I oversee critical responsibilities including newsletter design, website management, and organizing expert talks. As the GATE Coordinator, I ensure efficient administration of engineering entrance exams.

With expertise in MATLAB Simulink, battery modeling, LaTeX, and Python Programming, I actively contribute to research projects and skill development programs, enhancing educational quality. My extensive network (21,000+ LinkedIn followers) and leadership skills effectively bridge academia and industry, fostering valuable opportunities for students and faculty alike.
